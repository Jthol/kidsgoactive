Fests for the Family

Cream of Wheaton

June 5–8, 2025
Memorial Park & Downtown Wheaton

🎡 Carnival, kids’ activities, live music, business expo, arts & crafts, food vendors.

One of the biggest family events of the summer.

Scottish Festival & Highland Games

June 13–14, 2025
DuPage County Fairgrounds, Wheaton

🎶 Bagpipes, heavy athletics, dog agility shows, kids’ area, cultural demos, food, and a huge massed‑band finale.

Extremely family-friendly — lots for kids to watch and explore.

Naperville Fourth of July Celebration

July 4, 2025
Frontier Sports Complex, Naperville

🎆 Fireworks, food trucks, open fields for picnics.

Very family-friendly and easy parking compared to many fireworks shows.

Taste of Westmont

July 10–13, 2025
Downtown Westmont

🎵 Live music, carnival, kids’ zone, food vendors.

A great “street fest” vibe without being overwhelming.

Glendale Heights Fest

July 16–20, 2025
Camera Park, Glendale Heights

🎡 Carnival, fireworks, concerts, family entertainment.

One of the best mid-size suburban fests for kids.

Cantigny Summer Concert Series & Family Days

Sundays in August
Cantigny Park, Wheaton

🎶 Outdoor concerts, lawn games, splash pad, playgrounds, food trucks.

Relaxed, picnic-friendly, perfect for families with mixed ages.

Wheaton Brew & Seltzer Fest (Family Afternoon Session)

August 3, 2025
Memorial Park, Wheaton

🎉 Live music, food, and a designated family-friendly early session.

Surprisingly kid-friendly before the evening crowd arrives.

Naperville Last Fling

Labor Day Weekend, Aug 29–Sept 1, 2025
Downtown Naperville

🎡 Carnival, kids’ entertainment stage, parade, food, games.

Technically late summer, but a huge family tradition.
